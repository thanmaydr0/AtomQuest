## Design intent

- Brutalist, heavy, monolithic login composition.
- Strong depth cues and pronounced surface contrast.
- 3D scroll illusion with floating and parallax.
- Keep the existing background animation as atmospheric energy.
- The page should feel like a cinematic object that the user moves through.

## Non-negotiables

- Do not remove the existing animation.
- Do not flatten the page into a generic auth card.
- Do not break auth flow or existing routing.
